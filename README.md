NetFood 🍴
NetFood is an Android application designed to help users check the ingredients of food products using their phone's camera. By leveraging image recognition and ingredient analysis, the app provides a quick and easy way to identify what’s in your food, making it ideal for people with dietary restrictions, allergies, or those who simply want to make informed food choices.

Features
Ingredient Scanning: Use your phone's camera to scan food labels and extract ingredient information.
Allergy Alerts: Get notified if the scanned product contains allergens based on user preferences.
Nutritional Insights: Analyze the nutritional content of food items.
Search History: Keep track of previously scanned products for future reference.
User-Friendly Interface: Simple and intuitive design for a seamless user experience.
Technologies Used
Programming Language: Java/Kotlin (for Android development)
Framework: Android SDK
Image Recognition: OCR (Optical Character Recognition) for text extraction from food labels
Backend: Firebase (or any other backend service for storing user preferences and data)
Database: SQLite (for local storage of search history)
APIs: Integration with food databases for ingredient and nutritional information
